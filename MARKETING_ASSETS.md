### FAQ

**Q: Does my mom need to download an app?**  
A: No app required. She just texts back to a number. We can also set up a simple web page with two big buttons.

**Q: What if she doesn't have a smartphone?**  
A: Works on any phone that can receive texts (flip phones included).

**Q: Is this HIPAA compliant?**  
A: We use HIPAA-grade security, but we're a wellness app (not a healthcare provider), so HIPAA doesn't apply.

**Q: Can multiple family members get alerts?**  
A: Yes! Invite as many caretakers as you want.

**Q: What if there's an emergency?**  
A: Call 911. Bearable Senior is for daily wellness, not emergencies.
